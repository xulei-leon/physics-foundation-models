# Research Plan v1.0.0

## Research question

> Under a fixed \(H\to ZZ^*\to4\ell\) preselection and a validated
> mass-decorrelation protocol, how much does an XGBoost classifier improve
> expected profile-likelihood sensitivity over a cut-based baseline, and is
> the improvement stable across final states, seeds, and limited systematic
> variations?

## Scope and claim boundary

The corpus is the public ATLAS 13 TeV, 36 fb\(^{-1}\), 2015+2016 education
release. This release supports education and outreach; it does not expose the
full detector calibrations, object definitions, systematic variations, and
collaboration validation needed for an experiment-grade measurement.

The permitted conclusion is an internally reproducible comparison within this
open-data study. Discovery, precision cross-section, coupling, and official
ATLAS performance claims are out of scope.

## Hypothesis and endpoint

The primary hypothesis is that XGBoost, followed by a fixed DDT conditional-CDF
mapping, improves expected profile-likelihood significance over a cut-based
baseline without failing the mass-sculpting gates.

The primary endpoint is:

```text
expected_significance(XGBoost-DDT) - expected_significance(cut-based)
```

Secondary endpoints are weighted ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, background rejection,
\(\hat\mu\), its confidence interval, final-state stability, and variation
across five fixed seeds.

## Dataset

The formal inputs are the `exactly4lep` subsets of:

- data record `atlas-93924`;
- simulation record `atlas-93928`.

The file catalog is generated by direct HTTPS, stores explicit URLs, sizes, and
SHA-256 checksums, and is frozen before dataset construction. Unknown simulated
samples fail closed. Nominal signal requires a public name containing `H125`
and either `ZZ4l` or `ZZ4lep`; alternative generators for the same production
mode are systematic studies, not duplicate nominal yield.

## Fixed event selection

1. Require exactly four electrons or muons.
2. Require ordered transverse momenta above 20, 15, 10, and 7 GeV.
3. Require \(|\eta_e|<2.47\) and \(|\eta_\mu|<2.7\).
4. Require tight identification, loose isolation, zero total charge, a
   trigger-matched lepton, and an electron, muon, or dilepton trigger.
5. Build two disjoint same-flavour opposite-sign pairs. Choose \(Z_1\) as the
   pair closest to 91.1876 GeV; use lexicographic lepton indices for ties.
6. Require \(50<m_{Z_1}<106\) GeV, \(12<m_{Z_2}<115\) GeV, and all
   same-flavour opposite-sign pair masses above 5 GeV.
7. Retain \(105\le m_{4\ell}<160\) GeV.

The data interval 120--130 GeV is blinded. Data sidebands are 105--120 and
130--160 GeV.

## Models

- Cut-based: fixed physics baseline.
- Logistic Regression: linear baseline.
- XGBoost: primary classifier.
- scikit-learn MLP: neural-network control.

No deep-learning framework or particle-cloud architecture is part of v1.

The formal seeds are 17, 42, 314, 2026, and 2718. Hyperparameters are tuned
once on validation events with seed 42, frozen, and reused. The formal
classifier output is the event-aligned mean of five seed predictions; seed
variation is reported separately.

## Data separation

Simulation events are assigned by the SHA-256 hash of dataset identifier, file
checksum, and entry index to 70% train, 10% calibration, 10% validation, and
10% test. The same bucket rule is applied independently within each dataset.

Real events have no target and are prohibited from training. The calibration
split is reserved for DDT fitting. Validation is the only split used for the
one-time hyperparameter decision. Test events are untouched until the frozen
evaluation.

## Mass decorrelation and unblinding gates

The conditional background CDF is fitted on calibration-background simulation:

\[
s_\mathrm{DDT}=F_B(s_\mathrm{raw}\mid m_{4\ell},\mathrm{channel}).
\]

Bins begin at 5 GeV and merge in fixed adjacent order when effective sample
size is below 200. Low and high categories are `[0,0.8)` and `[0.8,1]`.

Every gate is mandatory:

- \(|\rho_\mathrm{Spearman}(s_\mathrm{DDT},m_{4\ell})|<0.05\) for background
  simulation and data sidebands;
- high-category background acceptance in every valid sideband bin is
  `[0.15,0.25]`;
- background-only spurious signal is below \(0.2\sigma\).

Failure blocks analysis freeze. The model or threshold is not automatically
changed after a failure.

## Reproducibility and reporting

All formal outputs are immutable, hashed, schema-validated artifacts.
Scientific results must trace through configuration, catalog, dataset
manifest, split manifest, run record, predictions, DDT calibration, templates,
freeze, and fit result.

Five seeded values, their mean, standard deviation, and standard error are
reported. Planned results remain explicitly marked as planned until retained
artifacts exist.
